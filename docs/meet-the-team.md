Manufactured Motion consists of three collaborators: Daniel Iacobacci, Zack Schwid, and Dillon Kane. The three of us all attended Binghamton University where we earned our B.S. in Computer Engineering, and our M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering. When we weren't in the classroom we often found ourselves dedicating our spare time to two different robotics clubs (The Binghamton University Rover Team, and the Watson Combat Robotics League). Through these efforts we began to strengthen our technical skills and develop a passion for robotics -- a passion which inspired us to create this project and begin designing our own robots!

Although we spent a lot of time together, we still have very different experiences and skillsets. If you are interested in learning more about us or the previous projects we have worked on, check out our LinkedIn profiles!
